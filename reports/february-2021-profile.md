## Reading These Results

The quantity field is a daily aggregate net balance. Summing it across dates measures aggregate **reported balance observations**, not unique failed trades. The dollar figure is only a descriptive proxy (`quantity × SEC reference price`) and is not a loss, liability, or transaction value. Review flags generated by the SQL layer are leads for investigation, not allegations.
