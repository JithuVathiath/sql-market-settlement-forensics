# Interpretation Boundary

## What The Project Can Say

- A security appeared in the published aggregate balance on a date.
- Its reported quantity was high relative to its own preceding observations.
- The market-wide distribution was more or less concentrated.
- A CUSIP appeared on consecutive observed settlement dates.
- The published ticker or description associated with a CUSIP changed.

## What The Project Cannot Say

- which firm or market participant caused a fail;
- whether the sale was long or short;
- how old any component of the aggregate balance was;
- whether two adjacent balances contain the same underlying failures;
- whether a review flag demonstrates manipulation, abusive short selling, naked short selling, fraud, or misconduct;
- whether the SEC reference price matches another market-data source.

Review flags are triage devices. Any substantive claim would require independent trade, clearing, corporate-action, and regulatory evidence that this public dataset does not contain.
